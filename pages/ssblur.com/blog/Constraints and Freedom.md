A while ago, I played around with the 
[PICO-8](https://www.lexaloffle.com/pico-8.php) a little.
Even ended up making a game, PUZZGRIND!!

That was a blast! Working in the PICO-8 is nice, to me, because oftentimes when I want to do something, there are constraints involved. 
PUZZGRIND in particular didn't present me with too many, but I was thinking
about things like optimizing the total number of tokens, 
how to design a scene without using a ton of different tiles,
operating within color limits, the works.

Those limits are fun! 

But moving on to my first real Lua project since, 
making a game with LÖVE, and man.

man.

Sometimes it is really nice not to have any of those limitations.

I guess it's a light-and-shadow thing.
Without experiencing those constraints, 
I wouldn't have any appreciation for game dev without them.

Regardless of the reason, though, I feel light as a feather. 
It feels like any time I want to do something, 
I can more or less just do it immediately, no fuss.

[LÖVE](https://love2d.org) has been a delightful engine
(probably more of a library) to work in,
handling most of the rendering and packaging stuff and just letting me run wild.

This perhaps wasn't the best composed,
but if there's any takeaway:
## Play with limits and then remove them

It's fun!
